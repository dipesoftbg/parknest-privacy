ParkNest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

ParkNest ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile application that helps you save and recall your parking location. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Location data
When you tap "Detect my location", the app requests access to your device's GPS to record the coordinates and street address of your parking spot. Location data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to any server operated by us.

Photos
If you choose to attach a photo to a parking spot (via camera or photo library), that image is copied to your device's local storage and associated with the saved spot. Photos are never uploaded to external servers by us.

Notes and text
Any notes, floor numbers, or zone labels you enter are stored locally on your device.

We do not collect names, email addresses, account information, or any data that identifies you personally.

2. Data Storage

All parking spot data (coordinates, addresses, photos, notes) is stored locally on your device using an embedded database. This data is never sent to our servers because we do not operate any servers. The app works fully offline after installation.

The app stores a maximum of 10 parking spots. When this limit is reached, the oldest entry (including any associated photo) is automatically deleted from your device.

3. Advertising

ParkNest displays interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalised ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • App usage information

On iOS, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before enabling personalised ads. If you decline, non-personalised ads are shown instead. On Android, consent is requested via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable for ParkNest

For more information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can permanently remove all ads by purchasing the Remove Ads in-app purchase. After purchase, no advertising SDKs are invoked and no ad-related data is collected.

4. In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We do not handle payment information directly. Purchase records are managed entirely by the respective store platform and persist across reinstalls on the same account.

5. Third-Party Services

When you open a saved location in an external map app, the app constructs a URL with the saved coordinates and hands it to the operating system. Depending on which app you choose, the following third parties may receive those coordinates:

  • Google Maps
  • Waze
  • Apple Maps (iOS only)

Map tiles displayed inside the app are provided by OpenStreetMap contributors and are loaded over an HTTPS connection.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored on your device. You can delete individual parking spots or all spots at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

ParkNest is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
